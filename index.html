Featured · AI Chrome Extension · Research

Harmonica

A text summarizer that lives inside your browser tab, backed by my published research. Press Ctrl+Q on any page, paste in an article, and get a short, readable summary without switching tabs. The model behind it combines BERT with adversarial learning, which keeps summaries coherent and on topic.

  • BERT + adversarial learning, 97.6% summarization accuracy
  • Published at IEEE ICTACS 2024: Abstractive Text Summarization Using BERT and Adversarial Learning
  • In-page popup with dark mode, no text length limit

What's public The extension front end is open source as Arctic. The BERT + adversarial model is private for now and will be released soon.
